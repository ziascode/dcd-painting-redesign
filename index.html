How we work

Prep is the job

Most of a great paint job happens before the paint. Here’s what to expect from first call to final walk-through.

Start with a quoteStart with a quote
  1. STEP 01

    Quote & colour advice

    We talk through your goals, the materials that suit them, and any other plans for the space, then give you a clear estimate. Ask us anything.

  2. STEP 02

    Protect & prep

    Floors and furniture covered, cracks filled, nail holes patched, surfaces sanded and primed. You can keep living around us.

  3. STEP 03

    Cut in, roll, spray

    High-quality products applied by hand or by sprayer, whichever gives the cleaner finish for that surface.

  4. STEP 04

    Clean up & walk-through

    A spotless work area every evening, and a walk-through at the end to touch up anything you want changed. You don’t need to be home in between.

Questions

Asked often

Can you help me choose the right materials?

Yes. We’ve worked across a wide range of materials and services. When we put your quote together, ask anything that comes to mind. The better we understand your goals and any other plans for the space, the more tailored our advice and recommendations can be.

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes, fully insured and bonded. Your property is protected in the unlikely event of an accident, and our crew is covered too, so everyone involved is safe.

Do I need to be home while you work?

No. We like to walk through the work with you before we start and again near the end, to make sure you’re happy. In between, your presence isn’t required.
